── about this archive

What this is

An automatically maintained archive of anime wallpapers. A scheduled job reads a curated set of community feeds once a day, filters out anything below the resolution floor, converts each image to WebP, extracts its dominant colour palette, and writes it into a static index. Nothing here is hand-tuned after ingest — the point is that the collection keeps growing without anyone babysitting it.

Sizes available

Wallpapers are grouped by aspect ratio: desktop (16:9 and 16:10, typically 1920×1080 up to 3840×2160 4K), ultrawide (21:9 and wider, for 3440×1440 and similar panels), and phone (9:16 and taller, sized for modern handsets). Use the numbered filters in the status bar, or press 1–4.

Why palettes

Every image is indexed by the colours actually in it, not by a tag someone typed. If you rice your desktop — Hyprland, i3, AwesomeWM, Waybar, Kitty, a Neovim colourscheme — you usually want the wallpaper and the theme to agree. Filter by hue to find a wallpaper that already matches your palette, then copy the extracted hex values straight out of the detail panel and drop them into your config.

Credit and takedowns

Each item keeps a link back to the post it came from and the account that posted it. Copyright stays with the original artists; this archive claims none of it. If you made something here and want it gone, open an issue on the repository and it will be removed from the next sync.
